During Delivery

What to Expect

Our delivery partner will call you 1–2 hours before arrival to confirm your address and timing.

✅ Check Before Paying

Inspect your books before payment. Verify titles, condition, and quantity. You can refuse if anything's wrong.

💵 Cash on Delivery

Pay the delivery person in cash. Get an invoice receipt. No online payment needed.

Common Questions

Delivery FAQ

❓ Can I track my order?
Yes! You'll receive an order confirmation email/SMS with tracking details. We'll also call you before delivery.
❓ What if I'm not home during delivery?
Our delivery partner will call you beforehand. Please coordinate a suitable time. If you're unavailable, contact us to reschedule.
❓ Can I change my delivery address?
Yes, if you contact us within 1 hour of placing your order. After that, the order is already assigned to a delivery partner.
❓ What if a book arrives damaged?
Refuse the damaged item during delivery. Contact us immediately with photos, and we'll send a replacement free of charge.
❓ Do you ship internationally?
Currently, we deliver only within India. We're working on expanding internationally soon!
❓ What if my order is late?
We guarantee 3–5 days within our service area. If it's delayed beyond that, contact us immediately for updates.
