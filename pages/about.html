About Buuks.in

We believe every reader deserves a personal book store. Our mission is to make discovering, browsing, and ordering books simple, delightful, and accessible.

Our Story

Building India's Favourite Book Store

A Love for Books, A Vision for Change

Buuks.in was born from a simple observation: finding the right book should be easy. Too many readers waste hours searching across platforms, facing outdated inventory, unclear delivery, or confusing pricing.

We set out to change that. In 2024, we launched Buuks.in as India's personal book store — a handpicked library where every title is curated with care, every purchase is frictionless, and every reader feels valued.

Today, we're proud to serve thousands of readers across India, from busy professionals to book clubs to passionate students. Our library continues to grow, and our commitment to making reading accessible never wavers.
