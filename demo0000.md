# 2 States: The Story of My Marriage

## Overview

2 States is a contemporary romance novel that captures the complexities of love across India's cultural and linguistic divides. Chetan Bhagat weaves together a heartfelt narrative about two individuals from different states—Tamil Nadu and Punjab—and their journey to unite their families through marriage. This engaging story celebrates the diversity of Indian culture while exploring the universal themes of love, family, and identity.

## Plot Summary

Krish, a young Tamil engineer from Chennai, meets Ananya, a confident Punjabi girl from Delhi. Despite coming from vastly different cultural backgrounds, they fall in love and decide to get married. However, their union faces unexpected resistance from both families, who hold deep-rooted traditions and values. What follows is a humorous yet touching journey as the couple navigates cultural stereotypes, family expectations, and societal pressures. The novel chronicles their efforts to bridge the gap between their two worlds and prove that love transcends regional boundaries.

## Key Themes

- **Love Across Borders**: A celebration of how love can flourish despite cultural and linguistic differences
- **Family Dynamics**: Exploration of family structures, parental expectations, and the evolving definition of family bonds
- **Identity and Belonging**: The characters grapple with their individual identities while finding common ground
- **Breaking Stereotypes**: The narrative challenges regional prejudices and promotes unity in diversity
- **Personal Growth**: Both protagonists undergo significant emotional and personal development through their journey

## Character Profiles

**Krish Malhotra**
Tamil software engineer with modern values, yet deeply connected to his roots. His pragmatic approach often conflicts with his mother's traditional outlook, creating tension throughout the story.

**Ananya Swamy**
Punjabi woman with strong personality and independent spirit. She challenges conventions and fights for her happiness while respecting her family's sentiments.

**The Families**
Both sets of parents provide comic relief while representing the real cultural barriers that exist in Indian society. Their gradual acceptance forms the emotional core of the narrative.

## About the Author

Chetan Bhagat is one of India's most bestselling authors, known for his ability to capture the pulse of modern India. His novels often blend humor with social commentary, making complex issues accessible to a wide audience. 2 States won critical acclaim and became a cultural phenomenon, later adapted into a successful Bollywood film starring Arjun Kapoor and Alia Bhatt.
